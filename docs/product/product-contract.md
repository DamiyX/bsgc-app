# Braid product contract

## Promise

Braid is not a WhatsApp clone or a popularity feed. It helps a person move through:

1. notice something in Scripture;
2. reflect privately;
3. deliberately share with one study group or accepted study contacts;
4. discuss and pray;
5. revisit what changed.

## Primary navigation

- **Today:** next study step, recent eligible contacts Insights, and private reflection entry.
- **Groups:** study circles and lifecycle/progress state.
- **Journal:** private searchable reflections and saved material.
- **Me:** identity, safety, privacy, notification, storage, legal, and account controls.

## Study-room spaces

- **Plan:** focus, dates, personal noncompetitive progress, completed recap.
- **Reflections:** observations, questions, and connections from study.
- **Discussion:** group conversation about the plan.
- **Prayer:** requests and prayers that should not disappear inside chat noise.

## Audience language

- **Only me**
- **Visible to {group name}**
- **Study contacts**

No control may use “public” for a contacts-scoped Insight.

## Healthy engagement

Measure completed study steps, accepted invitees who participate, substantive comments, and private reflections deliberately shared. Do not rank by likes, follower counts, referral-tree size, or post volume.

## MVP boundaries

Deferred until a complete contract exists: phone-book discovery, Google Drive backup/restore, video/documents, direct messages, unlicensed Bible translations, public discovery, leaderboards, web/desktop product releases.
