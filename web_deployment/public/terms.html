Braid Community Terms

Repository draft — July 28, 2026. Verified operator identity and jurisdiction-specific review are required before external beta.

Use with care

Braid is for Bible study, reflection, fellowship, and prayer. Do not harass, threaten, exploit, impersonate, spam, expose private information, or distribute unlawful or unsafe material.

Audience and safety

Check the audience before sharing and do not share another person’s private story, prayer, image, or recording without permission. Members can hide, block, and report. Braid may restrict unsafe content or access.

Account and invitations

Keep your account secure. Group owners manage roles and invites. Invite links can expire, be revoked, reach a use limit, or be refused for safety and capacity reasons.

Not professional advice

Braid does not replace medical, mental-health, legal, emergency, or safeguarding services.
