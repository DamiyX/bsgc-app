Delete a Braid account

  1. Open Braid and sign in.
  2. Open Me → Settings → Delete account.
  3. Complete the recent-authentication check and type DELETE.

If you own a group with other members, transfer ownership first. The server removes the account profile, private notes and state, authored Insights, account media, authentication record, and memberships. Shared messages are tombstoned so another member’s conversation is not corrupted.

A verified operator contact for deletion problems must be published before external beta.
