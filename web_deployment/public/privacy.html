Braid Privacy Notice

Repository draft — July 28, 2026. A verified operator contact and final retention schedule must be added before external beta.

Data used

Braid stores public identity fields; private sign-in data; per-device notification tokens; study membership and progress; messages, reflections, comments, reactions, reports, blocks, and invite-created connections. The MVP does not read or upload your phone book.

Visibility

Journal entries are private. Group content is available to current group members. Contacts Insights are available only to accepted Braid connections while active. Clients cannot list the global user directory.

Offline storage

Previously viewed data and media can be cached. Drafts and failed media uploads are stored locally per account. Sign-out clears private drafts, outbox entries, and image caches on that device.

Notifications and deletion

Notification categories and lock-screen previews are configurable per device. Account deletion is available in Settings after recent authentication; owners must first transfer shared groups.

External-beta requirement

The operator must publish verified contact details, retention periods, subprocessors, lawful bases, regional rights, child-safety position, and the final canonical notice before external testing.
